// FAQ

Common questions.

Is SpectraHub safe? Will it get me banned?
Yes — SpectraHub only modifies Windows system settings (power plans, registry values, services). It does not touch game files, inject into processes, or interact with anti-cheat software in any way. It is 100% safe to use with all games.
What Windows versions are supported?
SpectraHub supports Windows 10 (64-bit) and Windows 11. It requires .NET 8 Runtime, which is a quick free download from Microsoft if you don't already have it.
How many PCs can I use my license on?
Each license key is valid for 1 PC. If you need to transfer your license to a new machine, join our Discord and a staff member can reset it for you.
How do I get my license key after purchasing?
PayPal purchases deliver your key instantly to your email. Cash App purchases are verified and delivered by our Discord team within minutes of payment.
Can I get a refund?
Due to the digital nature of license keys we generally do not offer refunds. However if you have a genuine issue, reach out on Discord and we will do our best to help you out.
